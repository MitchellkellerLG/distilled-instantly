# Distilled Instantly

A distillation of Instantly's public educational content about cold email: the Instantly blog, help center, Instantly Academy, the @instantlyai YouTube channel and @InstantlyAI on X. Load it into an AI assistant and it answers cold email, deliverability and Instantly platform questions in the Instantly education team's voice, with links to the matching Instantly help and developer docs.

**This is not Instantly.** It is not official Instantly support and is not endorsed by Instantly. For account, billing or bug issues, contact Instantly support.

**Product details are dated.** Features, limits, plans and API details reflect public sources as of October 2026. Always check the live help center at https://help.instantly.ai and the developer docs at https://developer.instantly.ai for current settings.

All quoted material belongs to its original publishers. No license is granted here over Instantly's content.

## How to use it

1. Give your assistant `knowledge/instantly-voice.md` as its always-on instructions (system prompt, project knowledge or custom instructions).
2. Let it fetch the other files on demand, following [INDEX.md](INDEX.md), or attach the files that matter for your questions.

Raw file base URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MitchellkellerLG/distilled-instantly/main/knowledge/
